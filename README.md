# 🎨 ONYX Pigment – Public Website

**ONYX Pigment** is a public-facing website for ONYX PIGMENT PVT. LTD., a pigment manufacturer and exporter based in Gujarat, India.

The website showcases the company's pigment products, manufacturing focus, certifications, and applications across various industries. It helps visitors explore product information and connect with the company for business inquiries.

## 🌐 Live Website

**Visit Website:** [https://onyxpigment.com/](https://onyxpigment.com/)

## ✨ Key Features

- **Professional Home Page:** Introduces ONYX Pigment and its products.
- **About Us:** Provides information about the company, its mission, and vision.
- **Product Showcase:** Displays the company's range of pigment products.
- **Product Details:** Presents product information and applications.
- **Certifications:** Highlights company certifications and credentials.
- **Contact Us:** Provides contact information for business inquiries.
- **Quick Navigation:** Easy access to important website sections.
- **Business Information:** Displays company contact details and location.

## 🎨 Our Products

The website showcases four major pigment product categories:

1. **Copper Phthalocyanine Green**
2. **Copper Phthalocyanine Blue**
3. **Azo Pigment Red**
4. **Azo Pigment Yellow**

## 🏭 Industry Applications

ONYX Pigment products serve various industrial applications, including:

- Paints and Coatings
- Plastics
- Textiles
- Printing Inks

## 🏅 Certifications

The website highlights the following certifications:

- ISO Certification
- Chemexcil Certification
- IEC Certification

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objective of the ONYX Pigment Public Portal is to establish a professional online presence for the company.

The website helps visitors:

- Learn about the company and its products.
- Explore available pigment solutions.
- Understand product applications.
- View company certifications.
- Contact the company for product-related inquiries.
